# Opportunity Created trigger

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the trigger that starts a workflow when a new opportunity is created.

- Optional filters: Assigned To, Has Tag, In Pipeline, Lead Value, Lost Reason and Status.
- Tag filters support any-of and none-of style matching; several filters can be combined.
- Typical use: alert someone when a high-value opportunity is created.
