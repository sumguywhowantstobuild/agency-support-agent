# Registering an A2P campaign

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Step-by-step for registering a texting campaign in the Trust Center.

- Settings → Phone System → Trust Center → Brands & Campaigns → Campaigns tab.
- Needs the use case, realistic sample messages, how people opt in, website, privacy policy and terms.
- Review Application runs compliance checks that must pass before submission.
- Without a tax ID (sole proprietor) only one number can be registered.
- An approved campaign only works once the sending number is linked and shows A2P Verified.
