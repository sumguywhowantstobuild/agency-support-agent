# Customer Replied trigger

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the trigger that starts a workflow when a contact replies to a message.

- Filters include phrase matches, tags, intent, the workflow that was replied to, and Reply Channel.
- Reply Channel limits the trigger to SMS, email, social or chat replies; All-in-One Chat can be split into chat widget and live chat.
- Common uses: routing replies to the right team, alerting staff, creating opportunities from high-intent replies.
