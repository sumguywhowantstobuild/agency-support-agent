# Calendar Troubleshoot tool

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the built-in tool that explains why a calendar time isn't bookable.

- Open it from the wrench icon next to the calendar in Calendar Settings, or Troubleshoot Calendar in the calendar header.
- Pick a date and hover over a displayed unavailable slot to see a reason code, such as USER (no one available), CONFLICT (external calendar), BOOKED, BLOCKED, BUFFER, NOTICE, LOOKBUSY or PAST.
- It only shows slots generated from the calendar's availability and intervals: closed days and times outside them don't appear for inspection.
- It works in the logged-in user's timezone.
