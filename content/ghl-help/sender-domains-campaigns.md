# How sender domains work in LC Email campaigns

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains which domain campaign emails are sent from.

- On shared domains, free from addresses such as Gmail are accepted and adjusted behind the scenes to stay compliant.
- With dedicated domains, the from address must match the selected domain; mismatches warn or block.
- With several dedicated domains one can be chosen, or all used in rotation.
- The domain is locked once a campaign is sent or scheduled; unverified domains block sending. Applies to LC Email, not SMTP.
