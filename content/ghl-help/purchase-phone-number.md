# Buying a phone number

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Step-by-step for buying an LC Phone number and what to do afterwards.

- Settings → Phone System → Phone Numbers → + Add Number → Add Phone Number.
- Eligible +1 numbers (US, Canada, Puerto Rico) and Israel +972 numbers need Persona identity verification, generally completed once per sub-account; some countries need address documents.
- Purchases fail for missing verification, unavailable numbers, narrow filters, the wrong provider (Twilio vs LC Phone) or the wrong sub-account.
- Afterwards: configure call routing, test an inbound call, and complete any messaging registration required for the number type and where it texts (for example A2P 10DLC for US local-number business texting, or toll-free verification for toll-free texting to the US and Canada).
