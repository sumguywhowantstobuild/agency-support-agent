# Merging duplicate contacts

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains how to find and merge duplicate contacts, and what a merge does.

- Manual: Contacts → Smart Lists → select up to 10 contacts → More → Merge, then choose the master record.
- Manage Duplicates (three-dot menu on Contacts, admins only) finds likely duplicates by email, phone or name.
- Conflicting values can be reviewed; existing custom-field values on the master are kept and blank ones can be filled from the others.
- Conversations, notes, tags, tasks and opportunities are combined; check appointments, payments, orders and invoices first.
- Active workflow runs can move to the master or end if it's already in that workflow. Merges can't be undone.
