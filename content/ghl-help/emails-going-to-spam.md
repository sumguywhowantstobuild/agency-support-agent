# Why emails go to spam

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Lists common reasons emails land in spam and what to do.

- Sending from a free address instead of the business's own domain, or missing SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
- Poor lists (bought, old, unconsented), sudden volume spikes, spammy content, link shorteners, misleading subjects, no unsubscribe link.
- Reputation: spam complaints above about 0.3% or bounces above 5% hurt delivery; Google Postmaster Tools helps monitor it.
- Mail sent to the sender's own domain can be flagged as spoofing.
- Check authentication in Gmail with Show original.
