# A2P campaign rejections and how to fix them

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Lists common reasons campaigns are rejected and the fixes.

- See the reasons in Trust Center → Brands & Campaigns → Campaigns → View required fixes.
- Consent: SMS consent must be optional and separate, not pre-ticked, with message type, frequency, rates and STOP instructions.
- Website: must be public and show the business, contact details, privacy policy and the SMS program.
- Identity: details must match the registered business; sample messages need the business name and opt-out wording, without link shorteners.
- First check the rejection can be resubmitted: campaigns rejected for forbidden or high-risk content can't simply be corrected and resubmitted.
- Otherwise fix every listed issue before resubmitting; some fields need a new campaign.
