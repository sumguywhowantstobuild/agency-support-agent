# Workflow execution logs and enrollment history

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Covers the two tabs that show how contacts moved through a workflow and where they hit errors.

- Found in Automation → Workflows → open the workflow → Execution Logs or Enrollment History; also reachable from a message's details in Conversations.
- Statuses include completed, removed by a workflow action, removed by an external workflow action, contact merge and snapshot refresh.
- Errors are highlighted with a View Details link; Highlight Contact Path shows the steps a contact took, including errors and skipped steps.
- Date filters cover up to 30 days at a time; View Contact History shows a contact's actions across enrolments.
