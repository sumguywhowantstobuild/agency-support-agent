# Payment links with several products, test mode and expiry

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Covers advanced payment link options.

- Combine several one-time products and one recurring product; quantities, coupons, address collection and terms can be enabled.
- The Payment Mode toggle switches the link between Test (test cards) and Live (real payments).
- Automatic Deactivation sets an expiry date after which the link stops working.
- Preview before sharing; links can be sent by email or text.
