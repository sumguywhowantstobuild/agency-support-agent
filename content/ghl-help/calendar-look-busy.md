# Look Busy calendar setting

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the option that hides some open times so a calendar appears busier.

- Set on the calendar's Availability tab: switch it on and choose the percentage of open slots to hide.
- Only the booking page hides them; staff booking from inside GHL still see every slot.
- Turn the toggle off to show all slots again. Not available for class bookings or service menus.
