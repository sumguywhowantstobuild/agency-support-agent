# Form and survey submissions page

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the page where every form and survey response is listed, under Sites → Forms → Submissions.

- Columns can be chosen and reordered; each submission can be expanded to see all answers.
- A single submission can be downloaded as a PDF; selected submissions can be exported to CSV or deleted in bulk.
- There is also an export of all submissions to CSV.
- Bluejay support note: if the affected submission is recorded here, that submission was received; look at what happens to it next.
