# Workflow settings: re-entry, time window, stop on response

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the settings panel of a workflow and how each setting changes who enters and when messages go out.

- Allow Re-entry: whether a contact can enter again after they finished the workflow or were removed from it; a contact who is still active in it can't re-enter.
- Exception: appointment and invoice triggers give each appointment or invoice its own entry, whatever the re-entry setting.
- Allow multiple Opportunities: opportunity-based triggers start a separate workflow run for each qualifying opportunity; updates to an opportunity whose run is still active don't restart it.
- Stop on Response: ends the workflow for a contact who replies to one of its messages.
- Timezone (account or contact) and Time Window: messages scheduled outside the window are paused until it opens, which can look like a delay.
- Also sets default sender details and whether conversations are marked as read.
