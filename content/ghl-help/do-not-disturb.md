# Do Not Disturb (DND)

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains DND per channel and how opt-outs are handled.

- Outbound DND stops messages to the contact and can be set per channel (email, SMS, calls, Messenger, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp) or for all channels, on the contact's record or by workflow.
- Inbound DND is a single control covering calls and SMS together: it blocks the contact's calls and texts in GHL, though blocked incoming texts can still be charged.
- A STOP reply sets SMS DND automatically. Temporary DND can be changed on the record; a permanent opt-out needs the contact to opt back in (for example by texting START) or proof of consent sent to support.
- Certain listed operational emails (invoice and payment emails, calendar notifications, notifications) can bypass email DND; marketing emails stay blocked.
- The activity log shows who or what changed DND.
