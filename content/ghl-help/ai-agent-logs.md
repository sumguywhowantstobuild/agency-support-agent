# Conversation AI Agent Logs

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the logs that show step by step what a Conversation AI bot did.

- Open AI Agents → Agent Logs, or from a conversation or contact record.
- Each session shows the messages and the execution timeline, with timing and success or failure; when the bot calls a tool or action, the log shows which one and, for selected steps, what went in and what came back.
- To debug a booking, find the session, look at the booking step's inputs and result, and compare with what the contact asked for.
