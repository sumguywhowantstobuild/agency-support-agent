# How inbound calls are handled

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the whole path of an incoming call.

- An IVR or Voice AI can answer before any person; Voice AI can answer directly or act as backup.
- Calls from contacts assigned to a user, or to numbers owned by a user, can go to that user first.
- Team members answer in the web app (logged in), the mobile app (with calling permissions), on a personal phone or a desk phone; up to 6 extra members can ring at once.
- After the configured timeout (about 20 seconds is the suggested starting point), unanswered calls go to the chosen backup, such as Voice AI or voicemail.
- Call Connect makes the person answering confirm before the call connects; a whisper message tells them who's calling or why.
