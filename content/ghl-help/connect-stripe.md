# Connecting Stripe

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains connecting a Stripe account to take payments.

- Payments → Integrations → Connect with Stripe, signed in with enough access to the Stripe account.
- Check it shows as connected afterwards; reconnect if not.
- Payment methods are set separately for Test and Live modes.
- Only payments made through GHL after connecting are tracked; earlier Stripe payments aren't imported.
