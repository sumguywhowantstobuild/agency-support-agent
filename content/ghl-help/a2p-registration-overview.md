# A2P 10DLC registration overview

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains US registration for business texting from local numbers.

- Registration status is in Settings → Phone System → Trust Center: Pending, Approved or Rejected.
- Numbers are linked to an approved campaign in Phone Numbers and should show the A2P Verified label.
- After approval, carriers can take several business days to fully apply the registration.
- Toll-free numbers use a separate verification instead.
