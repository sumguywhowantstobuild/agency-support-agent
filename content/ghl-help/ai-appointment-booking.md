# Appointment booking with Conversation AI

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains how a Conversation AI bot books appointments inside a conversation.

- The bot collects the details it needs, checks the connected calendar's availability, offers slots and confirms the booking.
- Guided and Flow Builder bots book into a single calendar; prompt-based bots can choose between several.
- The calendar must be set up first; booking follows its availability and settings.
- Details the bot should discuss belong in the bot's prompt, not only in calendar descriptions.
