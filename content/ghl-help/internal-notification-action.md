# Internal Notification action

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the workflow action that notifies the client's own team by email, SMS, in-app notification or WhatsApp.

- Recipients can be the contact's assigned user, specific users, user roles, or extra email addresses (CC/BCC); duplicates across these are removed.
- With Assigned User, the step is skipped if there's no contact or no assigned user.
- A channel fails if it isn't set up for the recipient (for example no phone number for SMS).
- By default each user can receive up to 500 email, SMS or in-app notifications every 5 minutes; extra ones are queued and may be delivered after the limit resets or dropped.
- A step can show as processed even when delivery failed; HighLevel also mentions DND on the recipient's profile as a cause.
