# Conversation AI auto follow-up

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes automatic follow-up messages a bot sends when a contact goes quiet.

- Covers contacts who stopped replying, said they're busy, or asked to be contacted later; up to 5 follow-ups per case with set delays.
- When active hours are enabled, follow-ups go out only in the permitted hours, using the contact's or the business's timezone as chosen; with it off, they aren't limited by time.
- Changing how follow-ups are scheduled doesn't change how the bot handles its ongoing chat.
- Follow-ups are skipped for contacts who opted out, aren't interested or whose conversation has ended.
