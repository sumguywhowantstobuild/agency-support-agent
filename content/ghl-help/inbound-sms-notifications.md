# Getting notified about incoming texts

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains that incoming texts only land in Conversations and shows how to alert staff with a workflow.

- Call forwarding doesn't forward texts; a workflow is needed.
- Use the Customer Replied trigger with Reply Channel set to SMS, then an internal notification by SMS with the contact's name and message.
- Turn on Allow Re-entry so later replies can alert again once the contact has finished the workflow; a contact still active in it can't re-enter, so keep the workflow short.
- The alerts are outbound texts (they cost messaging fees), need valid staff phone numbers, and very long messages may fail.
