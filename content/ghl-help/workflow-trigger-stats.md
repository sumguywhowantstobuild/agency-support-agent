# Workflow trigger stats (Stats View)

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the trigger statistics in the workflow builder, used to see whether contacts qualified for a trigger.

- Open Stats View in the builder and select a trigger.
- Attempted = contacts the trigger checked; Matched = met every condition; Unmatched = didn't qualify.
- Clicking a number lists the contacts with the time and, for unmatched ones, the reason (for example a value mismatch or missing field), plus a summary of common reasons.
- Stats update within seconds but only cover the last 30 days; the workflow can't be edited in this view.
