# Call Connect

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the option that makes the person answering press a key before a call counts as connected.

- Enable it in Settings → Phone System → the number's three-dot menu → Edit Configuration → Advanced Settings: turn on Call Connect and Play Whisper Message, write the whisper prompt, and save.
- A whisper message asks the person answering to press a key; without a keypress the call isn't connected.
- Stops voicemail pickups on forwarded phones being recorded as answered calls, so call statuses and automations are accurate.
- Mainly needed for calls forwarded to outside phones, not calls answered in the GHL apps.
