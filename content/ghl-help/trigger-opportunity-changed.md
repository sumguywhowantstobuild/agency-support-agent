# Opportunity Changed trigger

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the trigger that fires on any change to an opportunity, wider than the status or stage triggers.

- Filters include Assigned To, Tag, In Pipeline, Lead Value, Lost Reason, Status and opportunity custom fields.
- Operators include has changed, has changed to a value, and equals.
- Older workflows built on this trigger keep their previous behaviour unless updated.
