# Managing Stripe payment methods

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains which payment methods customers see at checkout and why some are missing.

- Payments → Integrations → Manage next to Stripe → Manage Payment Methods.
- Test and Live modes are configured separately; check the right mode when a method is missing.
- Methods can be hidden by the Stripe account's country, currency, verification or the type of checkout.
- Apple Pay needs domain verification; Google Pay depends on the device and browser.
