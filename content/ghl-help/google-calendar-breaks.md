# Why the Google Calendar connection breaks

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains why a Google Calendar connection stops syncing and how to reconnect it.

- Common causes: the user changed their Google password, revoked HighLevel's access, Google blocked the access as suspicious, or the sign-in token expired.
- Symptoms: new appointments don't appear in Google, or the connection shows as disconnected.
- Fix: remove the connected Google account and add it again, then test; appointments already in GHL aren't lost.
