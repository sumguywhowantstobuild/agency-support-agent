# Filtering opportunities in a pipeline

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the advanced filters on the Opportunities page.

- Open a pipeline and use the Advanced Filter (funnel icon); filter by owner, status, source, dates, value, custom fields and more.
- Conditions can be combined with AND or OR logic, including nested filters.
- A pipeline shows only Open opportunities by default: a common reason an opportunity seems to be missing.
