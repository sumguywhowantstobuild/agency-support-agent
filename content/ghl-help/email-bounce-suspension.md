# Email bounce suspension

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains how high bounce rates suspend email sending.

- Sending can be suspended when bounces reach about 5% (0–3% is healthy), once enough emails have been sent: since an August 2026 update there is a tiered minimum send count and at least 25 bounces before an account is locked.
- The first two blocks last 12 hours; a third within the applicable escalation window is permanent until HighLevel support reviews it.
- The email verification service can lift temporary blocks early.
- Prevent it with list verification, double opt-in, a dedicated domain, smaller batches and sending to engaged contacts first.
