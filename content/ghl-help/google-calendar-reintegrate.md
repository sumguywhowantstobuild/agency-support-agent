# Re-connecting Google Calendar for a user

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Step-by-step for removing and re-adding a user's Google Calendar connection.

- Two places: Settings → My Profile → Calendar Settings, or Settings → Calendars → Connections; delete the account, then + Add New.
- The user who owns the Google account must do it; an admin using Login As can't.
- Upcoming appointments that didn't sync while disconnected are synced automatically after reconnecting.
