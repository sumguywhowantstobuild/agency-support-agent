# Checking an email's delivery in Conversations

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Shows how to check the reported delivery status of an emailed invoice.

- Open the contact's Conversations and find the email (envelope icon).
- The message's Details (three-dot menu) show what the mail server reported.
- Sent in the Invoices tab only means it was sent; the details show the mail server's result. Delivered means the recipient's server accepted it, not that it reached the inbox or was read.
