# How appointment workflows handle bookings, cancellations and reschedules

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains how appointment-triggered workflows behave across the life of an appointment.

- Each new booking can enter the workflow, whatever the Allow Re-entry setting says.
- Cancelled, invalid or no-show appointments remove the contact from the workflow.
- A reschedule removes the contact; they re-enter only if re-entry is on and a trigger for the rescheduled status matches.
- Recurring appointments don't start the Customer Booked Appointment trigger.
