# Viewing appointments in your own timezone

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the per-user setting for which timezone appointment times are shown in.

- Calendar Settings → Preferences → View Appointments In, then save.
- It only changes how times are displayed to that user, in the calendar, appointment list, contacts and conversations.
- It doesn't change availability, booking links or confirmations, and each user sets their own.
