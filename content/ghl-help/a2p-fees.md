# A2P 10DLC fees

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the costs of registering for business texting from local numbers to US mobiles through LC Phone.

- The first registration is a one-time bundle covering brand registration and campaign vetting, with fast-track processing that HighLevel estimates at about three business days.
- Each additional campaign under the same brand pays a vetting fee only, not the full bundle.
- A monthly fee per campaign depends on its use case, plus per-message carrier fees.
- HighLevel says the fees are passed through without markup; resubmitting a rejected campaign doesn't cost a new vetting fee.
