# Contact deduplication preferences

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the account setting that decides whether new submissions create a new contact or update an existing one.

- Settings → Business Profile → Contact Deduplication Preferences.
- Allow Duplicate Contact on: forms and Zapier create separate records even with the same email or phone; the article also covers Facebook and Instagram Messenger contacts.
- Off: GHL looks for an existing contact by the primary field (email by default, or phone), then an optional second field, and updates it.
- CSV imports are handled separately and follow the import's own matching (the article's wording on imports is not fully consistent).
