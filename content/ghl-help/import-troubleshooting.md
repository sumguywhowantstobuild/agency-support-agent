# Fixing CSV import errors

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Lists import error codes with their causes and fixes.

- Examples: 1008 invalid email format, 1013 phone not in international format, 1014 date format mismatch, 1005 and 1016 option values that don't match (or match ambiguously), 1004 a required property with an invalid value (that row isn't imported); for 1019 the article points to 1005.
- Some errors only leave a field empty while the contact still imports.
- Re-import the rows that failed; for contacts that imported with a field left empty, update that field instead.
- This article states a limit of 20 MB or 10,000 rows; another HighLevel article gives a different size limit.
