# Login As

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains how agency admins open a sub-account as one of its users.

- Profile icon → Login As → pick the user.
- Needs the Enable Login As permission, on by default for agency owners and admins.
- Useful for checking a user's access and settings; it skips the user's own sign-in.
- The article describes an initial release in which audit logs record the user's details rather than the admin's.
