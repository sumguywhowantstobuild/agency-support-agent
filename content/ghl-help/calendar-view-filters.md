# Calendar view filters

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the filters and options in the calendar view.

- The Meetings view covers all legacy calendar types, including legacy Service Calendars (v1) and Service Menus; it filters by Users, Calendars and Groups.
- The Services view is for Services v2 and filters by Staff, Services, Categories (and Location in some setups).
- The filter panel can be collapsed; day and week views can show buffer times.
- Google Calendar sync applies to Meetings only; Services v2 bookings are managed inside GHL.
