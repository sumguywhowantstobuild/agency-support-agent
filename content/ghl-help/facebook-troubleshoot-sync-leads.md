# Facebook Troubleshoot button and Sync Leads

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the troubleshooting tools on the Facebook integration card.

- Troubleshoot flags unmapped forms and permission problems and walks through fixing them.
- Sync Leads processes leads that didn't come through automatically, for a chosen time range such as 7 or 30 days.
- It covers all mapped forms on all connected pages at once, and is meant for exceptions, not routine use.
