# Common SMS error codes

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

A table of SMS error codes with what each means and what to do.

- Number problems: 21211 invalid format, 21614 not a mobile, 21635 landline, 30005 number doesn't exist, 30006 landline or unreachable.
- Opt-outs: 21610 means the recipient opted out or SMS DND applies; don't resend.
- Registration: 30032 toll-free not verified, 30033 A2P campaign suspended, 30034 number not fully registered.
- Filtering: 30007 means carriers filtered the message; look at content, consent, sender identification and carrier requirements, not just the wording.
- Limits: 30023 daily cap reached, 30002 account suspended, 30037 outbound disabled.
- Sender: 21606 means the From number is invalid or unavailable (check ownership, format, provisioning and restrictions); 21661 means the sending number has no SMS capability.
