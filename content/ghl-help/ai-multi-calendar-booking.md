# Conversation AI booking across several calendars

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Setup guide for letting a bot pick the right calendar from several.

- Set in the bot's appointment booking goal by choosing multiple calendars. Multiple-calendar booking is for prompt-based Conversation AI bots (see the Conversation AI appointment booking article).
- The bot matches requests using calendar names, descriptions and optional keywords.
- A fallback calendar catches requests that match none; it can be unchecked to have no fallback.
- The booked calendar is shown in the conversation and the appointment details; existing booking workflows keep working.
