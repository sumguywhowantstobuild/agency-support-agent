# Facebook lead ads not reaching GHL

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Troubleshooting guide for Facebook lead ads that don't sync.

- The person connecting must be a page admin, and that admin must keep access afterwards.
- The form must be mapped in Settings → Integrations → Facebook Form Field Mapping (blue tick).
- LeadConnector needs Leads Access: in Meta Business Settings → Integrations → Leads Access → Assign CRMs.
- Test with Meta's Lead Ads Testing Tool; reconnect if the connection expired.
- Workflows use the Facebook Lead Form Submitted trigger.
