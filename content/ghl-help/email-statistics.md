# Email statistics

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains where to see how emails performed.

- Campaigns: Marketing → Emails. Workflow emails: the statistics panel in the workflow. One-off bulk sends: Contacts → Bulk Actions, down to each recipient.
- Metrics include delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, failed, unsubscribed, complained and replied.
- Workflow stats default to the last 30 days; clicks and complaints can lag a few minutes.
- The article names Mailgun as supporting full metrics; other SMTP providers may show fewer.
