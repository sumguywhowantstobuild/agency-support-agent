# Why appointment slots are missing

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Lists the usual reasons a calendar shows fewer times than expected, with where to check each.

- Wrong calendar, or weekly hours and date-specific overrides blocking the day.
- No team member assigned to the calendar (then no times appear).
- Slot duration, interval and buffer that don't fit together; capacity limits on group calendars.
- Busy events on connected Google or Outlook calendars; timezone mismatches.
- The Troubleshoot tool (wrench icon in Calendar Settings) explains why generated slots are unavailable; a time outside the availability or interval settings isn't generated at all, so check those settings separately.
