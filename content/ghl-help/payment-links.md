# Payment links

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains how to create and share a payment link.

- Create a product first (Payments → Products), then Payments → Payment Links → create a link with the product and price.
- Only automatic payment methods (such as Stripe or PayPal) appear; manual methods like bank transfer don't.
- Links can prefill the customer's details and redirect after payment.
- The checkout's language follows the account's platform language.
