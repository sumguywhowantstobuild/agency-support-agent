# Facebook Lead Form Submitted trigger

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the trigger that starts a workflow when someone submits a Facebook lead ad form.

- Filter by Facebook page and form; without filters it fires for every form on every connected page.
- Form fields must be mapped first, or the data won't come through correctly.
- No forms appear in the filters until the page is fully connected with the right permissions.
