# Appointment List View

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Describes the list view of appointments under Calendars, useful for finding a specific booking.

- Open Calendars → Appointment List View.
- Filter by appointment status, assigned user, calendar, date or time, and contact details; filters combine with AND logic.
- Default lists include upcoming, cancelled and all appointments; custom lists can be saved.
