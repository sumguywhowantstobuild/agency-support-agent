# Missed Call Text Back

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains the feature that texts callers automatically after a missed call.

- Settings → Phone System → Voice → Voicemail & Missed Call Text Back → Missed Call Text Back; tick Enable and customise the message.
- It sends when a call rings past the incoming call timeout unanswered (10–20 seconds is recommended).
- Sending number order: the number called, one that texted this contact before, the default number, then any other eligible number.
- Needs an eligible SMS sending number, with A2P registration or toll-free verification where required; respects DND, and texts on every missed call, even repeat calls.
