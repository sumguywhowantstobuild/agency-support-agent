# Requesting a number that isn't available

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

Explains what to do when the number someone wants isn't in the search results.

- First try wider filters and nearby area codes.
- LC Phone accounts ask HighLevel support; Twilio accounts use Twilio's own request process.
- The article's week-based timelines (1–4 weeks) are examples for Twilio private offerings in Norway; get an estimate for the actual request.
