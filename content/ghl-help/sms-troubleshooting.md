# Troubleshooting SMS delivery

Our summary of a HighLevel help article (checked 2026-10-06). Open the source link for the full steps.

## Summary

A checklist for texts that fail or don't arrive.

- Find the failed message in Conversations (red status and error code), the contact's timeline, or workflow logs.
- Work out the scope: one contact, one number, or everything.
- Check the recipient (a valid mobile or message-capable number, correct format, not opted out or DND) and the sender (SMS-capable, with A2P or toll-free registration where required).
- Check account limits, suspensions and the content (length, attachments under 5 MB, links).
- Look up the error code in HighLevel's error dictionary. Recipients opt back in by sending a supported resubscribe keyword, such as START where applicable, to the same number.
